Plotwright revenue calculator

Compares three ways to fund the app each month: subscription tiers with ads on the free plan, open source with ads only, and open source with a one-time “remove ads” purchase. Every rate is yours to enter. Nothing here is a benchmark: eCPM, fill rate and conversion start empty on purpose. Same formulas as packages/core/src/monetization/revenue.ts.

Audience

Sessions: App Store Connect and Play Console report these. The multiplier lets you test “open source brings more users”; 1 means the same audience.

Open-source extras

GitHub Sponsors takes no fee on sponsorships from personal accounts and up to 6% from organisation accounts (GitHub Docs). The $4.99 price is a placeholder to change.

Per month

Ad revenue is what AdMob reports to you (its revenue share is already taken out and isn’t published). Store fees come off subscriptions and the remove-ads purchase. Taxes are not included.
