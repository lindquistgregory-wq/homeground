## App name
Plotwright

## Short description (80 characters max)
Plan your homestead on your own land: beds, animals and a step-by-step plan.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)
Plotwright turns your actual land into a homestead plan you can follow, from the first raised bed to the chicken coop, built from free public data about your property.

YOUR HOMESTEAD AT A GLANCE
Find your property and see it on sharp aerial imagery, with every bed, building, tree and pond you've drawn. Your animals walk around their coops and barns, and today's weather and tasks sit right on the map. Tap anything to see what's planted or living there.

A PLAN YOU CAN FOLLOW
Answer a few questions about your household, your time and your budget, and the planner sizes a garden and suggests animals to match. Your plan becomes a timeline of projects, like a vegetable garden, laying hens or honeybees. Each one is a short checklist: put it on your calendar and the steps get dates one after another. Check a step off early or late and the rest move with it. Finished steps add the routine chores they bring, such as collecting eggs or inspecting the hives.

KNOW WHAT TO PLANT, AND WHERE
• 137 vegetables, herbs, fruit and nut trees, berries, grains and forage crops, each scored for your climate and for the sun and soil in each bed.
• Suggested setups for every bed: plants that suit the spot and each other, with companion pairings, a mix of heavy and light feeders, and crop rotation in mind.
• Lay out beds on a square-foot grid, drawn to scale with each crop at its real spacing.
• A planting calendar with sowing, transplanting and harvest windows from your own frost dates, plus frost, heat and wind alerts from the National Weather Service forecast.

DESIGN YOUR WHOLE PROPERTY
Place raised beds, vineyard rows, orchards, greenhouses, coops, barns, rain barrels and more on real aerial imagery, drawn to scale. See how many hours of sun each spot gets, with your buildings and trees casting shade.

ANIMALS, IF YOU WANT THEM
25 species from hens and ducks to goats, horses and bees, checked against your land, with housing capacity, care dates and heat and freezing alerts. Name your animals, add photos, and keep free records for livestock and pets: health and vaccination dates with reminders, egg and milk logs, breeding due dates and a vet-ready history.

A PROFILE OF YOUR LAND
Boundary, slope and elevation, soils, flood zones, streams and ponds, and frost dates adjusted to your elevation, all from free public sources like USGS, USDA and NOAA. Every number shows where it came from.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account to create. What you enter stays on your phone, and the planner's AI runs on your phone where it's supported. We never see your property, records or photos.

The free plan covers one property and the essentials, with a few ads on browse screens. Grower and Homestead Pro subscriptions add more properties, the full planner, multi-year plans and budgets, with no ads.

Parcel lines from public GIS data are approximate and aren't a legal survey. Planting dates, step lengths and chores are planning guides; check local rules before keeping animals.
